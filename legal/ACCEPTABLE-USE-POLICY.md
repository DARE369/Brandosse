## 1. The short version

Do not use Brandosse to deceive people, to publish content you have no
right to publish, to harm anyone, or to do anything that would get us thrown off
a platform, a model provider, or a payment processor.

## 2. Content you must not create, upload or publish

- **Illegal content**, or content that promotes or facilitates a crime.
- **Child sexual abuse material.** Any instance is reported to the authorities
  and results in immediate, permanent termination without notice.
- **Non-consensual intimate imagery**, or sexualised depictions of real people.
- **Content that harasses, bullies, threatens or incites violence** against a
  person or group.
- **Hate speech** targeting people on the basis of a protected characteristic.
- **Content promoting self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders.**
- **Malware, phishing pages, or credential-harvesting content.**
- **Content infringing another person's copyright, trademark, design right,
  database right, publicity right or trade secret.**
- **Deliberately false claims about health, medicine, finance, elections or the
  law**, and any content presenting AI Output as verified fact when it is not.
- **Regulated-goods promotion** where you lack the licence to promote it, and
  content aimed at minors for age-restricted products.

## 3. Misuse of AI generation

You must not use the generation features to:

- **Impersonate a real person or organisation**, or create content that a
  reasonable viewer would take as genuinely made or endorsed by them.
- **Create synthetic media of a real, identifiable person**, except as permitted
  by section 3.1 below.
- **Fabricate evidence** — fake reviews, testimonials, endorsements, receipts,
  screenshots, credentials, news reports, or official communications.
- **Generate content designed to evade a platform's moderation**, including
  disguised text, cloaked links, or deliberately obfuscated claims.
- **Reproduce a copyrighted work or a distinctive artist's style** in a way that
  would infringe rights in your market.
- **Attempt to extract, reverse engineer, or bypass our prompts, system
  instructions, safety filters, or model configuration.**
- Do anything prohibited by the usage policies of the model providers listed in
  [`SUBPROCESSORS.md`](SUBPROCESSORS.md). Their restrictions pass through to you.

### 3.1 Depictions of real people

**You may generate depictions of yourself** — your own face, body or voice — and,
with their documented written consent, members of your own team or organisation
whose likeness you are authorised to use.

**You may not generate, or attempt to generate, a depiction of any other real,
identifiable person.** This includes public figures, celebrities, politicians,
competitors, and private individuals, whether or not the depiction is flattering,
and whether or not it is labelled as AI-generated.

If the Service produces an image resembling a real person you did not set out to
depict, that is an artefact of how the model works, not permission to use it. Do
not publish it.

## 4. Source Material and third-party media

**You may only submit a link to video you own.** We accept a link only where the
channel or account hosting that video is one you have connected to Brandosse and
which we have verified you control. Video published by anyone else is not accepted
from a link in any circumstance — including video you have licensed, video you
have been given permission to use, and video that is publicly accessible without
restriction.

If you hold rights in video that you cannot verify this way, **upload the file
instead.** Uploading is always available, is not restricted by this section, and
is governed by section 7.

You must also not:

- Submit a URL where retrieving the media would breach the source site's terms of
  service.
- Use the Service to build a library of other people's content, to systematically
  re-upload another creator's work, or to run a reaction or compilation operation
  on material you have not licensed.

We may block domains, refuse retrievals, and terminate accounts that repeatedly
trigger rights complaints.

## 5. Platform and publishing conduct

You must not use the Service to:

- Breach the terms, developer policies, automation rules or rate limits of any
  connected social platform.
- Operate **bot networks, engagement pods, or coordinated inauthentic
  behaviour**.
- Run **spam** — bulk unsolicited posting, comment spam, mass mentions, or
  repetitive near-identical posting across many accounts.
- Post to accounts you do not own or are not authorised to manage.
- Circumvent a platform's suspension, ban, or geographic restriction.

You are responsible for AI-disclosure labelling where a platform or your local
law requires it.

## 6. Technical conduct

You must not:

- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without our written
  permission, or breach any security or authentication measure.
- Access another user's or workspace's data.
- Scrape the Service, or access it by automated means outside a documented API.
- Circumvent Credit accounting, rate limits, or quota enforcement.
- Create multiple accounts to obtain additional free allowances.
- Resell, sublicense or white-label the Service without a written agreement.
- Impose an unreasonable load on the infrastructure, or use the Service for
  cryptocurrency mining or general-purpose compute.

## 7. Data you must not put into the Service

Do not upload or paste into a prompt:

- Payment card numbers, bank credentials, or passwords.
- Government identifiers — passport, national ID, social security numbers.
- Health records or other special-category personal data.
- Another person's confidential information you are not authorised to share.

The Service is not designed or certified for regulated data (for example PCI,
HIPAA, or equivalent) and must not be used to process it.

## 8. Reporting and enforcement

**Report abuse or a rights complaint to abuse@brandosse.com**, with enough
detail to identify the content and, for a rights complaint, a statement of your
rights and your good-faith belief that the use is unauthorised.

Depending on severity we may: remove content; disable a feature; suspend the
account; terminate it permanently; refuse refunds under the
[Refund and Credits Policy](REFUND-AND-CREDITS-POLICY.md); and report the matter
to law enforcement or the affected platform.

We aim to give notice and a chance to fix things where the breach is minor and
capable of cure. For the categories in section 2 that involve imminent harm or
illegality, enforcement is immediate.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy as platforms, model providers and law change. Material
