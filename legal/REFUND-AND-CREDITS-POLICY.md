## 1. How Credits work

Credits are prepaid units consumed when you run a job — a caption generation, an
image render, a video clip, a publish action. The Credit cost of each action is
shown in the Service before you run it.

**Credits are consumed when a job starts.** The compute is bought and spent at
that moment.

Credits are not money, are not a deposit, are not redeemable for cash, and earn
no interest.

**Expiry.** Purchased Credits expire 12 months after the date of purchase. Free
monthly allowance Credits expire at the end of the month in which they are
granted and do not accumulate.

## 2. Your rights under Nigerian law come first

Under the **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018**, if you are a
consumer you are entitled to a refund where a service is not rendered as agreed.
**Nothing in this policy limits that right, and no term here operates as a waiver
of it.** Where this policy and the FCCPA differ, the FCCPA wins.

Everything below is intended to be more generous than that statutory floor, not
less.

## 3. Automatic restoration — you do not need to ask

Credits are returned to your balance automatically when:

| Situation | Remedy |
|---|---|
| A job fails because of a fault on our side — an internal error, a provider outage, a timeout | Credits restored automatically |
| A job never reaches a final state | Credits restored |
| An individual clip fails to render while others in the job succeed | Credits for that clip restored |

If a restoration does not appear on your credit ledger, email
support@brandosse.com and we will correct it.

## 4. First purchase — 7 days, no questions asked

**On your first paid purchase, you may request a full refund within 7 days for
any reason at all.** You do not have to explain, and we will not ask.

- Available **once per account**, on the first purchase only.
- Credits consumed during those 7 days are deducted at the rate they were
  charged; the remainder is refunded in full.
- We absorb the payment processing fee. You receive the full amount you paid.

## 5. After the first purchase

| Situation | Position |
|---|---|
| Unused Credits | Refundable within 14 days of the purchase that created them |
| Consumed Credits | Not refundable, **except** where the Service failed to do what it described — see section 2 and section 3 |
| You did not like the generated Output | Not a refund ground on its own. Generation is probabilistic and quality is not guaranteed (Terms §9.2). This does **not** cover a job that failed, produced nothing, or did not do what the interface said it would |

| Your social platform account was suspended | Not refundable — outside our control |
| Expired Credits | Not refundable |
| Account terminated for breach of the [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.md) | Unused Credits forfeited, except where the law requires otherwise |

## 7. How to request a refund

Email **support@brandosse.com** from your account email address with the
transaction date, the amount, and — unless you are using the section 4 window —
what went wrong.

**We respond within 5 business days.**

## 8. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned **to the original payment method** through
Paystack. You do not need a Paystack account to receive one.

| Original payment method | Typical time to arrive |
|---|---|
| Card | 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank |
| Bank transfer | 2 to 3 business days |

**After 180 days.** Paystack can only reverse a transaction within 180 days of
the original charge. If your refund is approved after that window, we will pay it
by direct bank transfer instead, and will ask you for account details for that
purpose only.

**Currency.** Refunds are made in Naira, in the amount originally charged. We do
not compensate for exchange rate movement if your card was denominated in another
currency.

## 9. Chargebacks and disputes

**Please email us before raising a chargeback with your bank.** It is faster, and
in almost every case we will simply refund you.

If a chargeback is raised, we may suspend the account until the dispute is
resolved. Where a chargeback is raised after we have already refunded the same
transaction, or is found to have been made in bad faith, we may terminate the
account under Terms §15.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes are notified as set out in Terms §20,
and the version in force when you made a purchase governs that purchase.
