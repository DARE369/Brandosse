# Deep Launch-Readiness Audit — Brandosse
**2026-08-20 → 2026-08-21** · 117 findings · 11 pillars · Personal dashboard scope

---

# VERDICT: 🔴 NO-GO

Three independent grounds, **any one sufficient**:

1. **Live data exposure.** Any authenticated user can read other users' `posts` and `generations` — full content — with an ordinary JWT. Verified first-hand: 30 foreign posts from 7 users, including identifiable business content. Launching exposes real customers' unpublished work to each other.
2. **The core does not do what it claims.** Publishing reaches **one platform, one account, 6 posts lifetime**. Four accounts display "active" and cannot publish at all. Twenty posts have been frozen mid-publish since April. Buffer's *free* tier exceeds our paid capability.
3. **The differentiator is unimplemented.** Every loop handoff after "Platform → Analytics" is **BROKEN** with no carrier. No real engagement data exists anywhere; the one scheduled job that runs writes fabricated trends.

**Readiness: 44% by maturity, 0% by gate.** Only image generation (L3.5) reaches the L3 launch floor.

## The five things worth knowing

1. **Publishing is the keystone.** Analytics needs published posts; the loop needs analytics; reach needs the loop. Four pillars are blocked behind one.
2. **Fixing video generation makes unit economics *worse*.** It has never produced a video (1 real asset in 32 attempts), and at the cheapest credit tier the standard path loses money. **Reprice before repairing.** D5 recommends cutting it from v1.
3. **The system cannot detect its own failures.** `get_cron_job_status()` filters through a hardcoded 3-name allowlist and `healthCheck` calls it. Groq failed 100% for days with Claude silently absorbing the cost. Every major finding here was invisible until someone read the code.
4. **Every piece of content is generated by a 2024 model.** `_shared/llm.ts:51` defaults to `claude-3-5-sonnet-latest` and `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` is unset.
5. **Clipping is the strongest asset and the cheapest unblock** — genuinely competitive machinery gated behind two unset variables.

## Recommended path

**Completion Lockdown — ~19–21 weeks** ([D11](11-lockdown-plan.md)), then new implementation.

`Build the lock` → `Stop the bleeding` → `UI stops lying` → `Delete` → `Connect what exists` → `Complete the inadequate` → `Close audit gaps` → **🔓 lockdown lifts** → `Close the loop`

The governing rule: **a thing is LOCKED when it is fixed + proven + guarded.** In this codebase a fix without a detector silently un-fixes itself — Groq failed for days, trends were fabricated for five months, posts froze in April, all undetected. So the plan builds the locking mechanism first.

Cut video generation from v1. Ship *"clip your long-form video and publish it everywhere."* Then close one loop handoff — that single connection is what converts the thesis from a claim into a product.
